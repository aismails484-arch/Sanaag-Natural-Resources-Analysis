# Strategic Roadmap & Investment Tiers

### Phase 1: Research & Setup (6 Months)
* Site acquisition and environmental impact assessment.
* Procurement of initial laboratory and distillation equipment.

### Phase 2: Pilot Production (12 Months)
* First extraction cycles and chemical profiling.
* Development of fragrance prototypes and market testing.

### Phase 3: Global Scale (24 Months)
* Full-scale industrial production.
* Securing B2B contracts with international perfumery and pharmaceutical sectors.

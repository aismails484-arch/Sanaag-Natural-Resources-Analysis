# Technical Specifications: Essential Oil Laboratory
## Infrastructure and Equipment Framework

### Extraction Unit
* **Type:** Industrial Grade Steam Distillation System.
* **Material:** Food-grade Stainless Steel 316L (Anti-corrosive for high-acid resins).
* **Capacity:** Scalable modules for processing 50kg to 500kg of resin per cycle.

### Quality Control Instruments
* **Refractive Index Meter:** For immediate purity verification.
* **Specific Gravity Balance:** To measure oil density.
* **Storage:** Specialized UV-protected glassware and temperature-controlled storage environment.

### Production Standards
* Adherence to International Standards for Essential Oils (ISO 9235).
* GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) for cosmetic and pharmaceutical grade outputs.

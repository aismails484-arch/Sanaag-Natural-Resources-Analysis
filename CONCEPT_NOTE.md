# Concept Note: Frankincense Value Addition & Industrialization
## Focus: Boswellia Species of the Sanaag Region

### Executive Summary
This project outlines a strategic transition from raw resin exportation to high-value essential oil distillation. By integrating modern chemical engineering with traditional harvesting knowledge, the project establishes a sustainable industrial model in Somaliland.

### Academic Objectives
1. **Phytochemical Profiling:** Analyzing the chemical constituents of Boswellia frereana and Boswellia carterii.
2. **Distillation Optimization:** Determining the most efficient steam distillation parameters for maximum yield and purity.
3. **Sustainable Forestry:** Establishing a framework for resin harvesting that ensures tree longevity and ecosystem health.

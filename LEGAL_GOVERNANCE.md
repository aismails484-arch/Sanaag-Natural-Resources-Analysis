# Legal Governance & Regulatory Compliance
## Framework: Republic of Somaliland Statutory Regulations

### 1. Constitutional Foundation
This project operates under the supreme law of the **Republic of Somaliland**. All activities are aligned with the national commitment to economic development and resource sovereignty.

* **Article 12 (Public Assets):** In accordance with the Constitution, the project acknowledges that natural resources are public assets, and their industrialization must benefit the national economy through value addition.
* **Article 18 (Environment):** The state is responsible for the protection of the environment. This project commits to the "Green Extraction" protocol, ensuring that frankincense harvesting does not lead to deforestation or tree degradation.

### 2. Investment & Industrial Protection (Law No. 99/2021)
To ensure a secure environment for stakeholders and investors, the project adheres to the **Somaliland Investment Law**:

* **Article 15 (Guarantees against Expropriation):** Ensures that all private industrial assets, including laboratory equipment and processed oils, are protected by the state.
* **Tax Incentives:** As an industrial value-addition project, the initiative qualifies for customs duty exemptions on imported laboratory machinery and technical equipment (subject to Ministry of Finance approval).
* **Capital Repatriation:** Investors maintain the right to repatriate their capital and profits in convertible currencies as per national investment regulations.

### 3. Environmental & Forestry Regulations (Law No. 04/1998)
The industrialization of Frankincense in the Sanaag region is guided by the **Environmental Research and Disaster Preparedness Act**:

* **Sustainable Harvesting:** Implementing standardized "tapping" cycles for *Boswellia* trees to prevent over-exploitation.
* **Waste Management:** The laboratory will adhere to strict protocols for the disposal or recycling of spent resin biomass after the distillation process.
* **Biodiversity Conservation:** Active participation in the protection of the frankincense forest ecosystem in Sanaag to ensure long-term supply chain viability.

### 4. Trade & Quality Standards
* **Somaliland Quality Control Agency (SQCA):** All essential oils produced will undergo testing to meet national export standards before shipment.
* **Ministry of Trade & Tourism:** The project is registered and operates within the commercial guidelines of the Ministry, ensuring all international trade contracts are legally binding under Somaliland jurisdiction.

### 5. Local Community & Customary Law (Xeer)
While adhering to statutory law, the project respects the **Customary Law (Xeer)** of the local communities in Sanaag.
* **Land Use Rights:** Direct coordination with local cooperatives and landowners to ensure fair compensation and community-inclusive growth.
* **Dispute Resolution:** Any local operational disputes will be settled through a hybrid model of formal legal proceedings and traditional Somali mediation.

**Compliance Status:** Fully Aligned with Somaliland National Laws  
**Jurisdiction:** Hargeisa/Sanaag, Republic of Somaliland

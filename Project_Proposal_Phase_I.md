# Phase I: Resource Audit & Strategic Mapping
**Project:** Frankincense Industrialization (Sanaag)

## 1. Introduction
This phase establishes the scientific and logistical baseline for the project. Before industrial extraction begins, a comprehensive audit of the *Boswellia* forests in Sanaag is conducted to ensure supply chain stability.

## 2. Methodology
* **Field Survey:** Ground-level assessment of tree density and health.
* **Species Identification:** Classification of *Boswellia frereana* (Maydi) and *Boswellia carterii* (Moxor) populations.
* **Harvesting Protocols:** Evaluation of traditional tapping methods to align with international organic standards.

## 3. Deliverables
* Integrated Resource Map (Upcoming).
* Sustainability Compliance Certificate.
* Community Engagement Framework.

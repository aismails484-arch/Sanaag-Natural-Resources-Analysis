# Strategic Master Plan: Natural Resin Industrialization (Sanaag Project)
## Comprehensive Framework for Gums & Frankincense Development in Somaliland

### 1. Executive Summary
Mashruucani waa qorshe istiraatiiji ah oo ka kooban afar weji, kaas oo ujeeddadiisu tahay in la beddelo qaabka looga faa'iideysto khayraadka dabiiciga ah ee gobolka **Sanaag, Somaliland**. Waxaan diiradda saaraynaa in laga guuro dhoofinta agabka cayrin (Raw Resins), loona gudbo warshadaynta iyo soo saarista saliidaha qaaliga ah (Essential Oils) iyo cadarada (Fragrances) iyadoo la adeegsanayo tignoolajiyada shaybaarka ee casriga ah.

### 2. Project Phases & Roadmap

#### Phase 1: Foundation & Resource Mapping (Current)
* **Goal:** Inventory and legal structuring.
* **Activities:** Mapping *Boswellia* and *Commiphora* species distributions in Sanaag and establishing the **Institutional Legal Framework (Xeer)**.

#### Phase 2: Laboratory Infrastructure & R&D
* **Goal:** Scientific extraction and processing.
* **Activities:** Establishment of a state-of-the-art laboratory for steam distillation, chemical analysis, and perfume formulation.

#### Phase 3: Industrial Scaling & Branding
* **Goal:** Commercial production and identity.
* **Activities:** Scaling up extraction capacity and developing a premium "Somaliland Origin" brand for global markets.

#### Phase 4: Global Export & Market Integration
* **Goal:** International trade and logistics.
* **Activities:** Securing partnerships with international fragrance houses and establishing a robust supply chain for export.

### 3. Methodology & Academic Approach
Daraasadani waxay isku dhex daraysaa:
* **Botanical Analysis:** Hubinta tayada iyo hiddaha dhirta xabkaha soo saarta.
* **Chemical Engineering:** Adeegsiga hababka sifeeynta uumiga (Distillation) si loo soo saaro saliid saafi ah.
* **Economic Modeling:** Qiimaynta faa'iidada iyo saameynta dhaqaale ee uu mashruucu ku yeelanayo bulshada deegaanka.
